## Where this repo actually was

The `GeometryKernel` trait supported primitives (box, sphere, cylinder), boolean operations, transformations, and fillets. Fillets were added previously, but chamfers (beveled edges) and extrusions were absent from the kernel contract. The modeller lacked a 3D distance measurement tool and detailed sub-object face/edge inspection.
