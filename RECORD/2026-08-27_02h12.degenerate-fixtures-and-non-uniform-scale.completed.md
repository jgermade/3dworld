## Bugs found by building, not by reading

- **`gp_Trsf::SetValues` silent uniform scaling bug**: `gp_Trsf::SetValues` was passed non-uniform scaling matrices ($2.0, 0.5, 3.0$). Instead of throwing a C++ exception (`Standard_Failure`), `gp_Trsf` silently calculated the scalar cube root of the determinant ($\sqrt[3]{2 \cdot 0.5 \cdot 3} \approx 1.44225$) and applied uniform scale $1.44225$ instead! The `try/catch` block in `w3d_occt_transform` was never triggered, and `BRepBuilderAPI_GTransform` was never called. Adding explicit matrix inspection `is_rigid_plus_uniform_scale(m34)` in C++ fixed this defect and ensures `gp_GTrsf` is invoked.
