# Transparent Sub-object Selection & Edge Bar Overlay

## Where this repo actually was
Face hover and selection took lower priority in `fs_shade` WGSL shader than body selection (`object.selected`), causing any hovered or selected face on a selected body to be visually obscured by the body's orange highlight. Additionally, selected edges lacked persistent 3D bar rendering, and meshes without `line_positions` prevented `find_closest_edge` from detecting hover.

## Walkthrough · as built
1. **Dynamic Feature Edge Extraction (`Mesh::line_edges`)**:
   - Added [`Mesh::line_edges()`](file:///Users/joshua/dev/joshua/3dworld/kernel/src/lib.rs#L158) in `kernel/src/lib.rs`. If `line_positions` is empty, it automatically extracts topological face boundary feature edges from triangle face indices.
2. **High-Priority Shader Shading (`render/src/shade.wgsl`)**:
   - Updated shader branch evaluation order in `fs_shade`:
     1. Hovered edge (gold bar)
     2. Selected edge (orange bar)
     3. Selected face (bright cyan 85% mix)
     4. Hovered face (light blue 65% mix)
     5. Selected body (subtle 35% orange tint)
3. **Dual 3D Edge Bar Meshes in `Scene`**:
   - Updated [`Scene::update_edge_highlight`](file:///Users/joshua/dev/joshua/3dworld/app/src/scene.rs#L33) to maintain both `hover_edge_mesh` and `selected_edge_mesh` concurrently, ensuring clicked edges remain clearly marked in 3D space even when cursor moves away.
4. **Transparent Sub-object Selection**:
   - In [`Editor::picked`](file:///Users/joshua/dev/joshua/3dworld/app/src/editor.rs#L367), clicking on a face sets `selected_face`, populates the `Sub-object Inspector` with surface area and normal vector metrics, and highlights the face in bright cyan.

## Bugs found by building, not by reading
- `object.selected` pre-empted `in.face == object.selected_face` and `in.face == object.hovered_face` in `shade.wgsl`, rendering face hover and face selection completely invisible on any selected body.
