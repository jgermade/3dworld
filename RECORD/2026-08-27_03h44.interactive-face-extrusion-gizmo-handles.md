# 2026-08-27 03h44 · Interactive Face Extrusion Gizmo Handles & Direct Drag-to-Extrude

## Where this repo actually was

`PushPullFace` and `Shell` commands land in `core/`, `kernel/` and `app/`, callable via hotkeys (`P`, `H`) and Ribbon/Inspector buttons with fixed distance/thickness parameters. However:
- Gizmo handles only existed for body-level translation (`TranslateSelection`).
- Selecting a sub-object face in `SelectionMode::Face` did not render a 3D normal vector arrow handle on the face centroid.
- Interactive mouse dragging on a face normal handle to continuously extrude or push/pull a face along its normal was not implemented.
